OUR MISSION

Every child deserves the opportunity to become financially confident.

Financial literacy shouldn't be something students have to figure out on their own once they become adults. NextGen Wealth exists to introduce young people to the fundamentals of money early, giving them the knowledge and confidence to make informed financial decisions throughout their lives.

Through engaging, age-appropriate programs, we make financial education practical, approachable, and relevant to the real world.
